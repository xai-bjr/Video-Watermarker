Features

Everything creators need.
Nothing they don't.

Built for the daily grind of short-form posting. Drop in a folder, walk away, come back to platform-ready clips.

📥

Bulk import

Pick an entire folder — subfolders included — or hand-pick individual clips. VideoBulk queues them all and processes them back-to-back.

📐

One-click reframe

Convert any source to 9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, 4:5 portrait, or 16:9 widescreen. Six presets, one dropdown.

🖼️

PNG watermark

Overlay your logo on every clip. Adjust size, position, and opacity — perfect for a corner brand mark.

⚡

Never freezes

All rendering runs on a background thread. The UI stays fully responsive — no "(Not Responding)" dialogs, ever.

🎯

Three fill modes

Pad with bars, crop to fill, or blur the background — pick how mismatched aspect ratios should be handled.

📦

Fully portable

Ships as a standalone Windows app with FFmpeg bundled. No Python install, no dependencies — just unzip and run.

How it works

Four steps. Zero learning curve.

The whole workflow is laid out in numbered cards — no settings hunt, no hidden menus.

1

Pick your videos

Choose a folder or select individual files. Every supported video gets added to the queue automatically.

2

Add your logo

Pick a PNG watermark. VideoBulk places it in a corner of every clip. Skip this if you only need resizing.

3

Choose a format

Vertical for TikTok & Reels. Square for Instagram feed. Widescreen for YouTube. One click to pick.

4

Hit Start

Watch the progress bar fill as FFmpeg churns through every clip. Outputs land safely beside the originals.
